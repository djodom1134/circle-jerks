DATA & METHOD

Built to connect directly to Circle Jerks

1

Ingest

Aircraft tracks, tail numbers, touch-and-go detections, low approaches, timestamps, home-base inference, and dwell time.

2

Classify

Separate landings, touch-and-goes, local operations, outside training activity, and likely revenue-producing visits.

3

Price

Apply a user-selected lawful fee assumption consistently across billable events.

4

Translate

Convert gross opportunity into daily, monthly, annual, reserve, and project-funding views.

Concept prototype. Fee legality, collection design, exemptions, and exact operational classifications require City and aviation counsel review. The sample values in this demo should be replaced with live Circle Jerks API results.
